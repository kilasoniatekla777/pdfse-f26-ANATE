## Answers and exact quotes supplied by the user

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done.
“I'd moved and couldn't keep travelling to my old salon. I wanted a simple manicure before a family celebration, so I finally looked for somewhere local.”

### 2. Walk me through arranging that appointment.
“I asked a neighbor, searched two salons, and read their recent reviews. I called the one she recommended, explained that I prefer gentle cuticle care, and asked whether I could book a basic manicure.”

### 3. Where, if anywhere, did it become difficult?
“Deciding whether to trust a new place. Most photos showed finished nails, which didn't answer my questions about how they work. Some reviews were just a star rating with no explanation.”

### 4. What did that difficulty cost you in time or disruption?
“I spent about an hour researching across several evenings. I postponed booking for a few days because I wasn't sure, but I didn't miss anything important.”

### 5. What did you do to get around it?
“I asked specific questions on the phone about tools and cuticle treatment. Their answers sounded clear, and my neighbor had been there recently, so I booked a basic service to try them.”

### 6. How did another recent booking experience compare?
“I booked a haircut at my old salon while paying for the previous visit. That took a couple of minutes. I already trusted the stylist, so there wasn't a research stage.”

### 7. How much notice did you have in each case?
“Around three weeks for the manicure and six weeks for the haircut. Neither was urgent.”

### 8. How did you choose whom to contact?
“A recent personal recommendation mattered most. Then I looked for detailed reviews about the staff and how customers were treated.”

### 9. Did you reject any options? Why?
“I rejected a cheaper salon after reading several recent reviews about rushed service. Another only offered packages with gel, and I wanted a basic manicure.”
