## Answers

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
Last week. She was going to a friend's birthday and had to get ready the day before.

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
She wrote to a salon on Instagram asking if they had a free spot. Time passed and she kept waiting. They answered late: they had not seen her message, and the other times were already taken, so she could not come that day. She had to go very early the next morning instead, because the birthday was outside the city and she had to leave early.

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then?
The waiting was hard. She was nervous that she would be left without her nails done.

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
Covered in Q2: about one day, and she had to fit it before leaving early for the birthday outside the city.

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
She wanted shellac and strengthening, but there was no time left for the strengthening.

### 6. How did you choose the person or salon? Did you drop any option, and why?
She chose a salon close by, so she would not lose time on the road. She dropped one salon that did not send her photos of their work and had none on a website either; without seeing the work she did not trust it enough to go.

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What stayed unsure, and what did it cost you?
She went first thing in the morning, very early, and was nervous the whole time that it would not finish in time, because she was in a hurry.

### 8. What did you do to sort out the difficulty?
She could not sort it out. She got only shellac, because she did not want to go with untidy nails, and settled for that.

### 9. Tell me about another booking that was easier or harder. What was different?
It was easier when a salon had booking on Instagram. But there she could not see the location or compare prices and times with other salons. Still, being able to book directly was easy.

### 10. Who else booked nails recently that I could talk to? Can I contact you again later?
She told us to contact her friend. Asked if we can contact her again: yes, anytime.
