## Answers and exact quotes supplied by the user

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done.
“My shift changed, and I suddenly had Tuesday afternoon free. I'd been putting off a manicure, so I thought I'd use the gap before collecting my son.”

### 2. Walk me through arranging that appointment.
“I searched for nearby salons on Maps and called three. The first didn't answer, the second was full, and the third said they could fit me in at one thirty. I asked how long a basic manicure would take.”

### 3. Where, if anywhere, did it become difficult?
“Finding an actual opening. Search results told me who was open, but that didn't mean anyone had an appointment available. One salon suggested walking in without promising anything.”

### 4. What did that difficulty cost you in time or disruption?
“About twenty minutes of calls and checking distances. My free window was only a few hours, so I didn't want to spend part of it sitting in a queue.”

### 5. What did you do to get around it?
“I took the confirmed slot and chose regular polish instead of a longer service. I told them I needed to leave by two thirty before agreeing.”

### 6. How did another recent booking experience compare?
“I booked a massage the week before through a booking page. Choosing an available time was easy, although I still called to ask whether the appointment included consultation time.”

### 7. How much notice did you have in each case?
“A few hours for the nails, six days for the massage. I had much more choice for the massage.”

### 8. How did you choose whom to contact?
“Distance first, then recent reviews mentioning cleanliness and appointments starting on time. I wasn't looking for elaborate nail art.”

### 9. Did you reject any options? Why?
“I rejected the walk-in suggestion because I couldn't risk waiting. A salon across town had space, but the travel would use up too much of my afternoon.”
