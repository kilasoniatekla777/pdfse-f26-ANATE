## Answers and exact quotes supplied by the user

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done.
“My gel was growing out, and I had a client presentation on Thursday. On Monday evening, I decided I wanted an appointment after work on Wednesday.”

### 2. Walk me through arranging that appointment.
“I messaged my usual salon on Instagram and asked for Wednesday after six. They replied the next morning with two times, but one was during work. I asked about removal and the total appointment length, then confirmed the later slot.”

### 3. Where, if anywhere, did it become difficult?
“The back-and-forth was the awkward part. They answered the timing question but missed the question about removal. I couldn't tell whether the slot included enough time for everything.”

### 4. What did that difficulty cost you in time or disruption?
“I probably spent ten minutes writing messages, but the conversation stretched over a day. I held off arranging dinner because I didn't know when I'd finish.”

### 5. What did you do to get around it?
“I called during lunch. The receptionist added removal and said to allow about an hour and fifteen minutes. Then I put it in my calendar.”

### 6. How did another recent booking experience compare?
“I booked a haircut through another salon's booking page. I chose the service and time, and got a confirmation immediately. I knew the hairdresser already, so choosing someone wasn't an issue.”

### 7. How much notice did you have in each case?
“Two days for the nails, about ten days for the haircut. The haircut was easier partly because I had more flexibility.”

### 8. How did you choose whom to contact?
“My usual nail salon is near the office, and the manicures last well. I only look elsewhere when their hours don't work for me.”

### 9. Did you reject any options? Why?
“I rejected the daytime appointment because I'd have to move a meeting. Another salon had a later opening, but it was too far away to reach comfortably after work.”
