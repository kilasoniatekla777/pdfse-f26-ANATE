## Answers

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
“It was Tuesday afternoon, right after a long practice session at the piano studio. I was sitting on a bench taking a quick break, reviewing my sheet music, when I remembered my upcoming piano masterclass on Friday. The entire session was going to be professionally recorded with close-up shots of my hands on the keys. My professor had specifically mentioned that sleek, short black nails look striking and elegant on camera against the white piano keys, so I decided right then to book a manicurist to get them looking crisp and camera-ready.”

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
“Since I already have a go-to nail artist I trust, I pulled out my phone right there in the studio and sent him a quick text on WhatsApp. I asked if he had any open slots on Thursday afternoon before my big recording day. He texted back within about twenty minutes with a couple of options. I picked a 4:00 PM slot, texted him back to lock it in, and dropped a reminder into my calendar app so I wouldn't lose track of time while practicing.”

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then? If nothing was, what made it easy?
“Honestly, it was super easy! The process was practically seamless because I reached out two days in advance, giving us plenty of flexibility. The only tiny hiccup was that his 3:00 PM slot was already taken, but he offered 4:00 PM instead, which actually worked out even better for me because it gave me an extra hour of practice time at the piano.”

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
“I gave him about two days' notice. Fitting it into my schedule was very manageable—I just arranged my day so I could finish my heavy piano rehearsal blocks in the morning and early afternoon, leaving the late afternoon open for the salon before catching dinner.”

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
“When he confirmed the 4:00 PM slot, he let me know a full gel polish with precise filing and shaping for piano playing takes about an hour and a half, putting us at a 5:30 PM finish. Knowing exact timing made planning the rest of my evening effortless, so I booked a casual dinner with a friend for 6:00 PM right down the street.”

### 6. How did you choose the person or salon? What made you trust them? Did you drop any option, and why?
“I've been going to him for almost a year because he understands the unique needs of musicians. He files my nails to the exact short length required so they don't click against the keys while playing. I didn't even consider looking for another salon because finding someone who gets that technical detail right every time is rare.”

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What stayed unsure, and how much time did it cost you?
“Not at all! It was completely stress-free. The back-and-forth messages took less than two minutes of my attention, and nothing in my rehearsal schedule had to be canceled or pushed back.”

### 8. What did you do to sort out any difficulty? Can you show me the messages, with private details hidden?
“Since there were no real issues, confirming everything was straightforward.” Did not show the messages.

### 9. Tell me about another recent booking that was easier or harder. How much notice did you have, and what was different?
“Booking a piano tuning for the studio last month was definitely harder. I had to reach out a week in advance, coordinate between three different professors' teaching schedules, and negotiate around room availability, which took days of back-and-forth emails to finalize.”

### 10. Who else, outside your family, booked nails recently that I could talk to? Can I contact you again later?
“Another pianist from my conservatory got her nails done for a recital performance last week—I can ask if she'd be happy to answer your questions. And absolutely, feel free to reach back out anytime if you need anything else!”
