## Answers (translated from Georgian by GT)

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
“It was late Wednesday night, right before bed. I was in the kitchen fixing a late-night snack when I accidentally scraped my thumb against a counter edge and snapped a nail right down to the quick. I had a hangout coming up on Friday with a guy I really like, so looking down at my ruined hand while standing over my food made me instantly panic. I knew I couldn't show up to see him with my hands looking like a mess.”

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
“Since I already knew my sister's friend does nails, I pulled out my phone right there in the kitchen, opened WhatsApp, and shot him a message. I asked if he had any opening the next day after my work shift. He took a while to text back because it was late, but the next morning he sent over his availability. We went back and forth a couple of times before settling on a late-afternoon spot, and I saved it to my phone reminders right away.”

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then?
“Yeah, the main headache was that he didn't have any normal evening spots left on Thursday. He offered a spot much later than I wanted—right in the middle of my usual afternoon routine when I usually get chores done. I really wanted them ready for Friday's hangout with the guy and I trust his work, so I had to compromise and take a super inconvenient time.”

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
“I only had about 18 hours' notice. I had to scramble to restructure my entire Thursday schedule, rushing my afternoon errands and delaying dinner prep just to fit the appointment in.”

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
“When he confirmed the slot in the morning, he reminded me that fixing a broken nail along with a fresh set would take about two full hours. Knowing I wouldn't get out until after 7:00 PM forced me to cancel my evening workout and push off grocery shopping until Friday morning instead.”

### 6. How did you choose the person or salon? What made you trust them? Did you drop any option, and why?
“I went with him because he's a close friend of my sister. I've seen how precise his work is on her, so I knew he'd do a clean job before my hangout. I didn't even bother looking into commercial salons in the area because booking someone new on short notice feels risky when you want to look your best for someone you like.”

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What stayed unsure, and how much time did it cost you?
“It totally threw off my evening plans and created a lot of back-and-forth while waiting for replies. I spent about 20 minutes stressed out, checking my phone every few minutes until he finally locked in the time.”

### 8. What did you do to sort out the difficulty? Can you show me the messages, with private details hidden?
“I just accepted the awkward time slot to make sure my nails were done in time.” Did not show the messages.

### 9. Tell me about another recent booking that was easier or harder. How much notice did you have, and what was different?
“Booking a routine eye exam a month ago was way easier. I booked it two weeks in advance online through a portal where I could see a live calendar. I picked my exact slot in 30 seconds without texting anyone back and forth.”

### 10. Who else booked recently that I could talk to? Can I contact you again later?
“My sister books with him all the time, so she could definitely tell you about her experience booking with him. And sure, feel free to drop me a message whenever if you have more questions!”
