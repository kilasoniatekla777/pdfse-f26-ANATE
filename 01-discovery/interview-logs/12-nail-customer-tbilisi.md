## Answers

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
About two weeks ago. She was at work on her lunch break. Her nails had grown out and she wanted them tidy, and because she had to go back to work in the evening, she thought she might make it to a salon nearby during the break.

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
She searched Instagram for salons near her work and looked at several pages. She messaged one, asked if they had a free spot and said she wanted gel polish. They offered a few times; she picked one and they confirmed.

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then?
Yes, a bit. She messaged several salons and nobody answered right away. One answered much later, when the time they offered no longer worked for her. She got confused because she did not know where to go or who would have a free spot.

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
The same day: she remembered at work that her nails needed doing. It had to fit into her lunch break, and the salon had to be close to work because she could not travel far.

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
At first she did not know exactly what was included. When the salon answered, they told her the gel polish price, that removing the old material was charged separately, and roughly how long it would take. Strengthening would have taken a long time, so she decided to get only shellac and not add anything.

### 6. How did you choose the person or salon? Did you drop any option, and why?
Mostly by location and by photos of their work; she wanted a salon with clean, nice work. She dropped one that was further away, because going far from work did not suit her. In the end she chose the one nearby.

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What did it cost you?
Nothing big, but she lost part of her break for nothing while she waited for answers, and because of the waiting she had to rush a lot. She paid about 35 lari. She said it would have been good to see all the free times and prices from the start, so she would not have had to message several salons.

### 8. What did you do to sort out the difficulty? Can you show me the messages, with private details hidden?
She messaged other salons too, until one replied, and booked where they offered a free spot. In the messages she mostly asked whether they had a free spot, how much gel polish cost and where the salon was. Messages not shown. Later she did find a salon where she could see free times and book online, but by then it was too late to go: the search had taken too long.

### 9. Tell me about another booking that was easier or harder. What was different?
Easier: an earlier salon had its phone number on its page, so she called instead of messaging and sorted everything out and confirmed the booking in a few minutes. She still said it would be more convenient to see and choose free times online.

### 10. Who else booked nails recently that I could talk to? Can I contact you again later?
She suggested a coworker, who also recently had trouble finding a free time because of her work schedule. Yes, we can contact her again.
