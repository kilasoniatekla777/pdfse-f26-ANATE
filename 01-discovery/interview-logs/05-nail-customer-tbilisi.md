## Answers and exact quotes supplied by the user

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done.
“It was my usual maintenance appointment. I normally book the next visit before leaving, but a supplier meeting came up and I needed to change it.”

### 2. Walk me through arranging that appointment.
“I sent the salon a WhatsApp message asking to move Friday morning to Thursday afternoon. The receptionist offered Thursday at three. I said yes and asked them to keep me with my usual technician.”

### 3. Where, if anywhere, did it become difficult?
“The receptionist said it was booked, but my reminder still showed Friday. I didn't know whether the change hadn't been entered or the reminder was out of date.”

### 4. What did that difficulty cost you in time or disruption?
“It took another message and a five-minute call. I also briefly kept both times clear in my diary until they confirmed which one was correct.”

### 5. What did you do to get around it?
“I called and asked them to check the appointment book. Thursday was correct. They sent a fresh confirmation, and I deleted the old calendar entry.”

### 6. How did another recent booking experience compare?
“I moved a facial appointment using a link in its confirmation email. It showed the new date immediately and sent an updated reminder. That was easier, though I like talking to my nail salon when I have a special request.”

### 7. How much notice did you have in each case?
“The nails were originally booked four weeks ahead, and I changed them three days before. The facial was booked two weeks ahead and moved five days before.”

### 8. How did you choose whom to contact?
“I already knew where I wanted to go. I contacted reception because they manage the diary; I don't message my technician about appointments.”

### 9. Did you reject any options? Why?
“They offered another technician at a more convenient time, but I kept my usual person. She knows the shape I like, and I didn't want to explain it again.”
