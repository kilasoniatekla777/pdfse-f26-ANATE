## Answers

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
"Uh, Sunday night. I was at my keyboard, reviewing sheet music... I had a YouTube shoot coming up on Tuesday. Recording a piano piece. The camera is right over my hands, you know? Close-up on the keys. I looked down and saw two nails were cracked and the polish looked terrible. I was like, 'okay, I need this fixed tomorrow.'"

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
"I downloaded that app—Mindbody? A friend told me about it. Opened it, typed in nail studios near me. Found one called [studio name withheld] or something. Saw a 3 PM slot for Monday, tapped it, put my card in for the deposit, and that was it. Got a text confirmation right away."

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then? If nothing was, what made it easy?
"Not the app, but... the note part. In piano, if the gel is thick or long, it clicks on the keys. Microphones pick up every tiny sound. So in the app request box I was frantically typing, like, 'please make it ultra thin, no glossy topcoat, no noise.' Explaining that to a technician without talking to them face-to-face was kind of stressful."

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
"Less than a day. About 18 hours? I had a Zoom masterclass at noon on Monday and then rehearsal with my sound guy in the evening. Needed to squeeze it right in between."

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
"Right on the screen before paying. It said '50 minutes, natural buff and cuticle care.' So I knew I'd be out before 4. Once I saw that, I booked my taxi for 4:15."

### 6. How did you choose the salon or technician? Did you drop any option, and why?
"Just looked at reviews for 'cleanliness' and 'quiet atmosphere.' I thought about calling my usual home-visit girl, but she needs like three days' notice minimum, so I skipped her."

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What stayed unsure, and how much time did it cost you?
"Lost maybe 40 minutes on Sunday night just reading reviews about nail thickness and noise levels instead of practicing my piece."

### 8. What did you do to sort out any difficulty? Can you show me the messages, with private details hidden?
"Just that note box in the app. Here, look." *(Showed her phone screen: receipt showing the 50-minute slot and the note reading: "ultra-short, no shine, mic-safe finish". Not copied into the repo.)*
