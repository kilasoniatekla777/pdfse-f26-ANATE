## Answers and exact quotes supplied by the user

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done.
“It was my friend's birthday, and three of us wanted to get our nails done before dinner. We agreed on a maximum spend of forty each, including removal if needed.”

### 2. Walk me through arranging that appointment.
“I found a salon advertising a weekday offer and sent the details to our group chat. Then I messaged the salon asking for three appointments together. Each friend wanted a slightly different service, so I had to collect that information.”

### 3. Where, if anywhere, did it become difficult?
“The offer didn't include removal, and nail art was extra. Also, they had three spaces that afternoon, but only two people could start at the same time. I had assumed we'd all finish together.”

### 4. What did that difficulty cost you in time or disruption?
“About half an hour of messages between the salon and our group across two days. We moved dinner back by forty-five minutes so the third person wouldn't be rushed.”

### 5. What did you do to get around it?
“I asked for a total price for each person and the expected finishing times. We picked simpler designs, accepted staggered starts, and one friend paid the group deposit. We paid her back.”

### 6. How did another recent booking experience compare?
“I booked a lash appointment for myself through a booking link. The service had a clear price and duration, so it was quick. Coordinating three people was the extra work this time.”

### 7. How much notice did you have in each case?
“Eight days for the group nails and two days for the lashes. The shorter-notice appointment was easier because I only had to work around my own schedule.”

### 8. How did you choose whom to contact?
“Price came first, then location near dinner and photos of their work. I checked recent reviews before contacting them because none of us had been there.”

### 9. Did you reject any options? Why?
“One salon was over our budget once removal was added. Another couldn't fit all three of us in that afternoon. We also skipped an offer whose terms were unclear and whose messages didn't answer our price questions.”
