## Answers

### 1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
"It was last week, Tuesday morning. I was at home drinking coffee and reviewing my opening strategies because I had a neighborhood backgammon tournament coming up on Thursday. When playing backgammon, your hands are constantly on display—rolling dice, moving checkers, hitting blots—so my hands need to look sharp and feel comfortable. I noticed my nails were chipped and rough, and I couldn't go to the tournament like that."

### 2. Walk me through how you arranged it, from the first step to the end.
"First, I called my sister's friend on the phone, who does nails. I asked if he had a slot available before Thursday. He told me he had an open time on Wednesday morning. I said that worked for me, confirmed the appointment time over the phone, and noted it down."

### 3. Was any part of it difficult? What happened then? If nothing was, what made it easy?
"The difficulty wasn't the timing at all—the time slot was fine. The issue was communicating the exact functional shape and coating material I needed for playing backgammon. I needed the edges trimmed and rounded in a very specific way so they wouldn't catch or click awkwardly against the wooden board and checkers during fast plays. Explaining over the phone exact structural details like gel thickness and edge beveling was really difficult."

### 4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
"I gave about 24 hours' notice. I had to fit it around my morning household chores and my afternoon backgammon practice match with my neighbor."

### 5. When did you find out what was included and when the whole service would finish? What changed after that?
"When I sat down in his chair on Wednesday morning. I explained the checker-handling requirement in person, and he told me the full prep, custom rounding, and gel coat would take about an hour and fifteen minutes. Once I knew the exact end time, I called my backgammon partner to confirm our afternoon practice match for 2:00 PM."

### 6. How did you choose the salon or technician? Did you drop any option, and why?
"I chose him because he is my sister's friend and I know his work standard. I considered walking into a walk-in salon down the street, but I dropped that option. I didn't trust a stranger to handle the nail shape correctly without making them too thick or sharp for handling dice."

### 7. Did arranging it disrupt anything else? What stayed unsure, and how much time did it cost you?
"It disrupted my practice focus on Tuesday. I spent over an hour making phone calls, trying to explain what I needed, and worrying whether the nail length would interfere with my grip on the dice. I was unsure right up until I sat in the chair."

### 8. What did you do to sort out any difficulty? Can you show me the messages, with private details hidden?
"Since I couldn't explain the nail shape well on the phone, I took a picture of my backgammon set and a sketch of the nail curve I wanted, then sent it via message. Here, I can show you the message exchange." *(Showed her phone screen: photo of the backgammon board sent with voice notes and text detailing edge shape. Not copied into the repo.)*

### 9. Tell me about a time it went easily. (Easy counterexample, plus probes)
"Last month, I called him two weeks before a regional match. He remembered my exact preference for short, smooth edges, booked me right away, and finished in 45 minutes."

*Probe: strict deadline, appointment runs long, who and why stay?*
"If an appointment runs long right before a match, it ruins my mental focus and warm-up routine. I stay with my sister's friend because he listens to my specific requests for backgammon play, even when explaining the technical shape is tough."

*Probe: a time it was not easy at all?*
"Once before a major tournament, the technician applied a gel topcoat that was way too thick and square at the tips. When I tried rolling the dice, my thumb kept catching on the cup edge. I had to go back late in the evening and have him completely file them down."

### 10. Is there anyone else who has booked recently who might talk to me? (plus short-notice probes)
