## Script to read from · about 20 minutes

Ask permission to participate, take notes and publish anonymous quotes. Ask separately before recording. State they can skip questions or stop. Do not mention a proposed product before the experience questions.

1. Tell me about the last time you decided to get your nails done. Where were you, and what else was happening that day?
2. Walk me through arranging it, from your first step to the final outcome.
3. At which point, if any, did it become difficult? What happened next? If nothing was difficult, what made it straightforward?
4. How much notice did you have, and what did you need to fit the appointment around?
5. When did you learn what was included and when the entire service would finish? What changed afterward?
6. How did you choose the person or salon? What did you learn about the person or treatment that made the choice acceptable? Was there an option you dropped, and why?
7. If arranging it disrupted something, what other plan stayed unresolved, when could you finalize it, and what time or cost did it add? If nothing changed, record that explicitly.
8. What did you do next to resolve any difficulty? Can you show the steps with private details hidden, if comfortable?
9. Tell me about another recent booking that was easier or harder. How much notice did you have, and what was different?
10. Who else with a recent booking experience could we talk to? May we contact you again to clarify notes or show a later test?

Ask questions 1–3 before naming availability, duration or trust. Record what arose before the targeted questions. Optional follow-ups: “And then?”, “What happened last time?”, “When did you find that out?”
