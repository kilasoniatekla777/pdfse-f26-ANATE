# Problem Statement

## The statement

1. **Who:** Nail customers in Tbilisi who book a manicure that has to fit before a fixed commitment later that day or week: a presentation and dinner (Maya), collecting a son after a few free hours (Nina), a wedding design (Leila), or a birthday dinner for three friends (Amara). (01, 02, 03, 06)
2. **When:** The moment the salon offers a start time by message or phone, but the customer still doesn't know whether removal, the design, or everyone in the group will be finished before they have to leave. (01, 03, 06)
3. **Cost:** About ten minutes of messages stretched over a day with dinner plans on hold (01). Forty-five minutes over two evenings and a simplified design (02). Twenty minutes of calls inside a free window of a few hours (03). Half an hour of group messages over two days, with dinner moved back forty-five minutes (06).
4. **Today:** They call or message again to ask for the total length and price, then cut the service down until it fits: regular polish instead of a longer service, a simpler design, staggered starts. (01, 02, 03, 06)
5. **Why it fails:** The answer to "when?" arrives without the answer to "how long?", so the customer has to keep asking. "They answered the timing question but missed the question about removal. I couldn't tell whether the slot included enough time for everything." (01)

## What the evidence ruled out

We started out believing the hard part was waiting to find out whether any suitable time was free. Only 1 of 6 customers raised that unprompted (03). The other 5 raised a different main difficulty: removal and duration (01), the artist's skill (02), trust in a new salon (04), a conflicting reminder (05), and extras and group timing (06). Verdict: weakened in Week 3. See line 2 of `DECISIONS.md`.

## What is still open

Whether knowing the full finishing time before confirming would change where or when a customer books, or would only save a follow-up call. No log answers this, and we don't yet know how often it happens to the same person. Four of six customers weren't booking on the same day, so our ICP (Nino, a same-day booker) also needs re-checking against these logs.

## Pattern behind it

- Pattern: Customers need the complete service and finishing time clarified before a nominal opening is usable. (01, 02, 03, 06)
- Root cause: In these cases, an offered start time did not by itself establish that the complete requested service fit the customer's usable window.
