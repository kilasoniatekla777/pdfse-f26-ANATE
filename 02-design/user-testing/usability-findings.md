## After 3 sessions: apply the decision rule

**Sessions run:** 3 · **Passes:** 3 (sessions 1, 2, 3) · **Fails:** 0

**Rule says:** not decided yet. The rule is out of 5: 4/5 successes supports a real-provider feasibility test, and 2+ failures means revising the service and finish-time screens. With 3 passes, one more pass in sessions 4 and 5 reaches 4/5; two fails would mean revise. Run New-A and New-B, who haven't been interviewed before.

**Pattern so far:** nobody missed removal or picked a slot finishing after 14:30, and nobody needed to message the salon. That is the opposite of our predicted first mistake. Session 3 was close to the time limit, so speed is worth watching. All three testers named removal and gel afterwards; only Nina gave the exact finish time, so ask in exact words next time: "What exactly is included, and what time do you finish?"
