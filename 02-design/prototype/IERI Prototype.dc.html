IERI · Mobile prototype · Round 1

Discover, see real availability, book.

Three live phones on one shared calendar. Book Nail Studio Vake on either customer phone and it lands in the salon console. Cancel or block a time in the console and both customers see it straight away.

1a
Customer · list-first
Home is a feed of salon cards with their next real times. Map is its own tab. Slots use a day strip and a time grid.
1b
Customer · map-first
Home opens on the map with a list sheet under it. Tap a pin to pull that salon up. Slots are stacked by day, showing only bookable times.
1c
Salon console · Nail Studio Vake
One specialist's day at a time. Phone, Instagram and IERI bookings in one list. Tap a booking to cancel it, or tap a free time to block it.
