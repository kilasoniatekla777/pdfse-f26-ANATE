# Practice interview reflection · Anano Kutchava

Recorded October 18, 2026 from Anano's account. The interview date was not supplied. This test interview with my mother is practice and does not count toward the required real interviews.

My original question was approximately: “Do you think there is a problem with online booking in Tbilisi, and how comfortable would you be with it?” This wording is reconstructed from my recollection, not a transcript. It introduced our framing and asked for an opinion about a hypothetical solution, making it easier to get an answer in favor of the idea.

I changed the question to: “At which point, if any, did it become difficult? What happened next?” This asks about a specific experience, allows the person to say nothing was difficult, and follows what happened instead of asking them to approve our idea. The same approach appears in the team's [script v1](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v1.md) and [script v2](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md).
