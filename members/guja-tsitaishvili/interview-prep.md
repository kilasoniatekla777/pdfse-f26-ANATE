# Interview prep · Guja · Week 4 (interviews 07 onward)

Script: [interview-script-v2](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md). Log template: [log-template.md](log-template.md).

## Who to recruit (2 to 4 people)

Someone in Tbilisi who booked a nail appointment in the last few weeks, ideally around a fixed plan (event, dinner, pickup, work). Not in this course. Not a friend who knows our idea. Ask about the **past booking**, so a "short notice" requirement is a plus, not a filter.

## Recruiting message (do not mention the product or idea)

English:
> Hi [name]! I'm a student at KIU doing a course project about how people arrange nail appointments. I'm looking for people who booked a manicure recently to tell me how it went, whether it was easy or annoying. It takes about 20 minutes, in person or on a call, and I'm not selling anything. Would you have time this week?

Georgian (check wording with a native ear before sending):
> გამარჯობა [სახელი]! KIU-ს სტუდენტი ვარ და საკურსო პროექტისთვის ვაგროვებ ინფორმაციას, როგორ იწერებიან ადამიანები მანიკიურზე. ვეძებ ადამიანს, ვინც ბოლო დროს დაიჯავშნა მანიკიური და მომიყვება, როგორ ჩაიარა, მარტივი იყო თუ არა. დაახლოებით 20 წუთი დამჭირდება, პირადად ან ზარით, და არაფერს გყიდით. გექნებათ ამ კვირაში დრო?

## Consent line (read aloud, record the answer in the log)

English (from script v2):
> "I'm a KIU student learning how arranging nail appointments works. This takes about 20 minutes. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes while we talk? (If solo: "if I take notes".) I'll use a code name in our course repository and may include anonymous exact quotes. You can skip any question or stop at any time."

Then ask separately: "Is it okay if I include anonymous quotes?" If they say no to notes or quotes, stop the logged interview. **No recording.**

## Pair up

Course rule (Lab 2, running-and-logging): one person asks, one logs, never both. Ask Tekla or Anano to join as logger. The logger writes verbatim quotes and, at the end, asks one question you missed. If nobody can join, the log must say "from memory" and be written straight after. You must be **asker** in at least 2 logs for hw1.

## Rules to keep in the room

- Questions 1 to 3 first. Do not say "availability", "waiting", "how long it takes" or "app" before they raise it.
- Ask about the last time, not what they would do. If they say "I usually…", ask "tell me about the last time".
- Do not pitch. If they ask what it's for: "a course project about booking, I'll tell you more after."
- If they say "someone should make an app": don't describe our idea. Ask "What would it need to do for you?", then straight back to the past: "When did you last feel that?"
- Silence: count to three before speaking.
- Write quotes in their words (Georgian original plus an English translation labelled as such).
- Close with: who else booked recently, and may we contact you again.
- Write the log and commit it within 24 hours, one commit per interview, numbered `07-…`, `08-…`.
- Note the moment you led them or pitched; you need one Mom Test slip for hw1.md.
