## Week 4 · The prototype is the conversation

**Where I applied it:** I ran my first two real interviews with script v2, both as asker: [log 07](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/07-nail-customer-tbilisi.md) and [log 08](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/08-nail-customer-tbilisi.md). I never mentioned the idea and only asked about what had already happened.
**What I did faster than planned:** I got the second interview the same night. The person in 07 referred their sister, who books with the same technician, and I called her that evening.
**What I noticed:** The same technician gave two very different experiences. With 18 hours' notice, 07 waited overnight for a reply and only learned the two-hour duration at confirmation, which cancelled a workout. With two days' notice, 08 got a reply in twenty minutes and planned dinner around the finish time. Notice time, not the technician, seems to make the difference.
**My slip:** In 08 I asked the participant to "be honest" because she is close to her technician. That does not remove the bias; next time I will ask for facts like message and reply times.
