## The idea

Help customers in Tbilisi find beauty professionals, compare service information, discover an available appointment, and reserve it. Help salons and independent professionals keep appointments received through multiple channels consistent.

## Assumptions to test

- Waiting to learn whether a time is free is a significant part of the booking cost.
- This happens to more people than the one observed customer.
- Tbilisi contains a reachable initial customer group.
- Customers need a different workaround rather than already having a fast, reliable preferred technician.
- Providers could keep availability accurate. This requires separate provider interviews and subsequent use evidence.
