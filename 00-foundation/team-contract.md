## How we work

* **Channel:** ANATE Microsoft Teams group
* **Standup:** Sunday at 18:00, 15 minutes max
* **Response window:** messages acknowledged within 12 hours between 09:00 and 22:00
* **Silent teammate with a deadline near:** The teammate responsible for the relevant task follows up after 12 hours. If there is still no response after another 12 hours, the issue is raised in the team channel.
* **Demo-ready:** any member can screen-share the current product state in any lab, on 2 minutes notice

## Contribution tracking · proposed addition for team review, 2026-10-07

Track actual work through Git commits from each member's own account, asker/logger fields in interview logs, decision owners in DECISIONS.md, and each member's weekly pattern journal. At the Sunday standup, review these records and agree the next actions. This addition and the outreach-field repair do not imply renewed sign-off by either member; both should review them.

## How we decide

Small stuff: any two members. Direction: evidence first, then majority; the dissent gets one sentence in the record. From Week 3, product decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` with a link to their evidence. That file is the team memory; keep it honest.

## When it breaks

1. Name it in the team channel within 48 hours of noticing. Silence is how last year's teams collapsed.

2. Still stuck after one standup: raise it at a lab checkpoint.

3. Involves everyone, or step 2 failed: email [zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge](mailto:zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge) for mediation.
